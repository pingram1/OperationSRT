Description: (8) Two-variable equations and relationships--foundations of linear relationships. The student applies mathematical process standards to use proportional and non-proportional relationships to develop foundational concepts of functions. The student is expected to:
(A) determine the constant of proportionality (k = y/x) within mathematical and real-world problems;
(B) distinguish between proportional and non-proportional situations using tables, graphs, and equations in the form y = kx or y = mx + b, where b ≠ 0; and
(C) identify examples of proportional and non-proportional relationships that arise from mathematical and real-world problems.
