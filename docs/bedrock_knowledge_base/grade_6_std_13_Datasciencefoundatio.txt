Title: Data science--foundations of measurement and data. The student applies mathematical process standards to represent and analyze data. The student is expected to:
Description: (13) Data science--foundations of measurement and data. The student applies mathematical process standards to represent and analyze data. The student is expected to:
(A) distinguish between situations that yield data with and without variability; and
(B) represent numeric data graphically, including dot plots, stem-and-leaf plots, histograms, and box plots.
